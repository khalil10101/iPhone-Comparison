Top 10 iPhones

This is my own ranking with my own notes, based on my experience and my modest knowledge.

1

iPhone 16 Pro

This is literally the best iPhone ever made in my opinion it has everything for a good price and a very good design and a very firm body

2

iPhone 17

This is for my fellow people who don't like the pro model, this phone has what the pro model has but at a more affordable price it sits at around 8000Dh this is my go to if the pro is not my thing

3

iPhone 14 pro

I'm in love with this phone it has around 70% of what the newer pro model has, it can do any task you throw at it, strong with a gorgeous design and very strong.

4

iPhone 16 Pro Max

This is the largest iPhone in the ranking, offering the biggest screen and battery life for those who need them and honestly although my hate for the pro max lineup if I was offered to choose one this what it would be .

5

iPhone 15 Pro

This is a solid pick if you want a type-c port and 8gb of ram but i don't see much why you would take this over the 14 pro but still a good phone overall

6

iPhone SE (2022)

If I personally were on a tight budget this would be my go to, I'm so in love with that small design and that iconic button, well it's not actually a button but still

7

iPhone 13 Pro

I rather go with the 14 pro but still this is a great deal, although I heard some rumors that some of them have a screen problem, you might want to check with that before buying one

8

iPhone 18 Pro

Honestly, I'm not sure if this phone is worth it compared to the older pro models but if you have money, or you're coming from an old iPhone I understand and if so this is the best deal you could have .

9

iPhone 15 Pro Max

Except the occasional heating problem, this is a great deal. But I would wait a little bit and save up to buy the 16 pro max

10

iPhone 12 Pro

Safest option, you're not getting the latest features, but it's a solid phone overall.
